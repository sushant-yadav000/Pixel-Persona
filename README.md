## 🧠 About The Project

**THE UNSEEN** is a creative frontend project developed for **Pixel Persona at Babu Banarasi Das University (BBDU), Lucknow**.

Built around the theme **“Student Life Pressure”** and the pressure point **“The Truth About Student Life,”** the project highlights the struggles students often hide behind their everyday smiles.

Academic expectations, family pressure, financial worries, comparison, career uncertainty, and fear of failure can affect students in ways that are not always visible to others.

The goal is to turn these invisible struggles into an interactive digital experience that encourages awareness, self-reflection, and healthier coping strategies.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🎭 **The Unseen Reality** — Explores the difference between how students appear on the outside and how they may feel inside.
- 🧠 **Student Pressure Scenarios** — Highlights common challenges involving academics, family expectations, friendships, and career decisions.
- 📊 **Interactive Pressure Meter** — Represents student pressure through a visual and engaging interface.
- 💬 **Hidden Struggles** — Brings attention to emotional challenges that students often hesitate to discuss.
- 🌱 **Practical Coping Strategies** — Presents ideas for managing stress, organizing priorities, taking breaks, and seeking support.
- 🎨 **Immersive Dark UI** — Uses a cinematic dark aesthetic with modern visual elements to create an engaging experience.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Designed with the goal of providing a smooth experience across different screen sizes.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students are often expected to perform well academically, build successful careers, meet family expectations, and maintain a balanced social life.

But who notices the pressure behind all these expectations?

**THE UNSEEN** aims to make student struggles more visible and remind people that success is not the only thing students are dealing with.

## 💡 Our Approach

Instead of creating a conventional informational website, this project uses an interactive and visually engaging frontend to communicate the emotional reality of student life.

The experience combines visual storytelling, relatable scenarios, interactive elements, and practical suggestions to encourage students to acknowledge their struggles and take small steps toward managing pressure.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** — Page structure and content
- **CSS3** — Styling, animations, layouts, and visual effects
- **JavaScript** — Interactions and dynamic UI behavior

No frontend framework is required for the core project.

## 🌍 Impact & Purpose

This project is designed to:

- Encourage conversations about student stress.
- Help students recognize common sources of pressure.
- Promote healthy habits and constructive coping strategies.
- Encourage empathy among students, educators, friends, and families.

**The message is simple: Every student has a story beyond their grades.**
